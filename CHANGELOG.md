# Changelog

What changed on the site and in its data, newest first. Spotted a mistake? [Report it on GitHub](https://github.com/zenisor/poe-atlas-diff/issues/new?template=data-mistake.yml).

## 12 November 2026: the rest of the patch notes

The Endgame, League, Item generation and Monster sections of the [3.30.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013680), paraphrased and sorted by mechanic.

- The Overview has a new **Outside the tree** section with the Atlas-wide changes. League mechanics now come from Threads, Item Quantity on Maps became Atlas Drops, Rarity now decides what drops, Map juice gives less Rarity, and the tree has 150 points.
- Every mechanic page lists its **Other 3.30 changes** (71 notes in total). Examples: Harvest without the Sacred Grove, Ritual vessels that need Ritual Threads, and Ultimatum rewards that keep improving after round 10.
- Strategies show the changes for every mechanic in the setup, under the notables.
- More scarab notes: the three Harvest scarabs, [Ritual Scarab of Wisps](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Ritual?focus=ScarabRitual2), [Ultimatum Scarab of Bribing](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Ultimatum?focus=ScarabUltimatum2), [Bestiary Scarab of the Herd](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Bestiary?focus=ScarabBeastsNew2), and where the new Allflame Embers drop.

## 12 November 2026: patch notes pass

Every scarab was checked against the [3.30.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013680) (Endgame and Atlas sections) and the wiki.

### Scarab fates

- Three scarabs are no longer marked Gone. Their effect is now an **Allflame Ember**, a new 3.30 currency you use on a Map:
  - [Divination Scarab of The Cloister](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Divination%20Cards?focus=ScarabDivinationCardsNew1) is now Allflame Ember of the Cloister.
  - [Cartography Scarab of the Multitude](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Maps?focus=ScarabMapsNew5) is now Allflame Ember of the Multitude.
  - [Scarab of Divinity](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Rare%20Monsters?focus=ScarabMisc3) is now Allflame Ember of the Divine.
- [Ambush Scarab of Containment](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Strongboxes?focus=ScarabStrongboxNew4) is now **Only a chance on the tree** (was Partly). The scarab worked like Monstrous Treasure. In 3.30, Explorer's Reliquary gives Strongboxes in Tier 16 Maps a 10% chance to be the Empyrean Apparatus, which drops a Tier 16 Monstrous Treasure Map. It was linked to Hidden Horrors before; that node is now listed as related.
- New totals: 34 gone (was 37), 4 partly (was 5), 8 only a chance (was 7), 3 now an Allflame Ember.

### Outside the tree

- [Delirium Scarab](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Delirium?focus=ScarabDelirium1), [Beyond Scarab](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Beyond?focus=ScarabBeyond1) and [Scarab of Wisps](https://zenisor.github.io/poe-atlas-diff/#/scarabs/Viridian%20Wildwood?focus=ScarabMisc8) get a brass corner in the stash: Rare Maps can now roll a suffix with their effect (a Mirror of Delirium, an Evocator, the Viridian Wildwood).
- 13 more scarabs got a note about related Endgame changes: Delirium mirrors and fog, Rogue Exiles, the 8-modifier Vaal outcome for Maps, Tormented Heretics, Shrines, Essences and Allflame Embers.
- Thread scarabs now say that primary mechanics no longer appear in Maps on their own, and that Shaped Regions give free Threads.

### Display

- Effect names keep numbers that every node shares, like "Tier 16 Maps" instead of "Tier X Maps" (28 names).
- Tooltips no longer lowercase names, so "Now comes from Threads" keeps its capital.
- The 3.30 tree is labelled 3.30.0. GGG's `3.30.0` tag points at the same commit as preview 2, so no tree numbers changed.

## 12 November 2026: first release

- Compares the 3.29.3 and 3.30.0 Atlas trees from GGG's export and shows what each of the 116 scarabs became.
- Pages for the overview, each mechanic, scarab fates, notables and keystones, all tree effects, the tree map and farming strategies.
- Search, phone layout and the current look were added the same day.
